The RaaS math

Formic's pitch to a plant manager, as live math. One station, staffed versus automated. Move the levers a factory would move.

The assumptions: 2,080 hours per shift per year, wages fully loaded. The savings matter, but the removed risk is the sale: no capex approval, no integration project, no robotics engineer on payroll. A factory that could never buy automation can rent it.
