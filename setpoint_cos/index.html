The memo

Yesterday's pricing meeting ran 94 minutes and ended without a decision. This is what the exec team had in their inbox by 6pm.

Decision memo: per-loan-file pricing for verification and calc-agent work
Drafted from the meeting transcript and the pricing model, edited by a human once · one page · decision requested by Friday
Situation

Flat platform fees leave money on the table with the largest facilities and price out the smallest originators. Two term sheets stalled on this in the last quarter.

Options discussed

A. Keep flat fees. Simple, predictable, and the current model. Caps revenue on the ten largest facilities and loses the long tail.

B. Per-loan-file pricing with a facility minimum. Revenue scales with the volume verified and calculated. Modeled on current volumes it is accretive on 31 of 38 facilities, and the seven where it is not are grandfathered for twelve months.

Recommendation

Option B, new deals only, effective September 1. The modeling is attached, the grandfather clause removes the churn risk that stalled this conversation last quarter, and deal desk can quote it Monday. If the seven grandfathered facilities renew at usage rates next year, this is worth roughly 12% of ARR.

The ask

A yes or no by Friday. On yes, I own the rollout: deal desk scripts, the two stalled term sheets get revised quotes first, and we review actuals at the January WBR.

Decision captured in the log either way · follow-through tracked on the initiative board above
