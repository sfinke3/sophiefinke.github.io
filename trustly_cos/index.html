Q3 business review · one-page draft

Assembled from the two modules above · narrative edited by a human, once

Programs: five of six Q3 program milestones hit. The returning-user checkout flow is the quarter's standout, live to 50% of traffic with conversion up meaningfully where deployed. Bank coverage closed the Q3 target late but closed it. The failover test finally ran after we protected two engineers from the release rotation for a sprint, which is a resourcing pattern worth keeping.

Hiring: 22 starts against a Q3 plan of 24, with the gap concentrated in staff-level engineering. Offer accept rate held at 76%. The two aged offers from September both closed after comp was escalated inside 48 hours, and that faster escalation path is now standing policy.

Carried risks: production data access for model validation remains the slowest dependency in P&T, three programs touched it this quarter. A scoped-access framework is drafted and sits with security.

Asks for Q4: a decision on the scoped-access framework, and one incremental recruiter through year-end to de-risk the Q4 hiring tail.
