An AI Chief of Staff, not another dashboard.

Sapien's argument is that a dashboard reports while an AI that learns your business does the work.1 A lot of the Chief of Staff job is that kind of work, like the board update, the investor email, and the weekly leadership digest, each written from numbers that live across different systems. So I built a version of that coworker. It takes a plain request, pulls the numbers from the tools, writes the deliverable, and flags the parts that need a person. The role also asks for real dashboards, so it builds one of those too. Pick a task below to see it run.

If I had the job

First 30 / 60 / 90 days

The fastest way to show what I would do in this role is to run the founder's office on Sapien itself. I would point the Company Engine at our own systems so the board updates, investor notes, and leadership digests draft from source, with every number traceable and the judgment calls flagged for a person.

First 30 days

Learn and instrument

  • Sit in every function's staff meeting and map how decisions get made and where they stall.
  • Stand up the real-time leadership dashboards the role calls for, covering revenue, deployment health, and operational KPIs in one shared source of truth.
  • Dogfood Sapien by pointing the Company Engine at our own NetSuite, Salesforce, and Slack, so board, investor, and leadership comms draft from source and trace back to it.
  • Take board-pack and exec-meeting prep off the founders' plates entirely.
Days 30–60

Tighten the operating layer

  • Install one weekly operating rhythm built on a single source of truth, a single decision log, and OKRs that are honest and owned.
  • Own the fundraise data room and the metrics narrative end to end.
  • Put gross-margin and onboarding-backlog reviews on a standing cadence with named owners and dates.
Days 60–90

Make it compound

  • Turn the dashboards from reporting into action, so when a number breaks plan the coworker drafts the board note, the customer email, or the fix and routes it for a person to decide.
  • Run the quarterly planning and reforecast.
  • Extend the AI coworker to the next jobs, like customer QBR prep and recruiting updates, so the operating layer scales without adding headcount.
