Built for the Chief of Staff role at Runway

A world model of the company

Runway's argument is that trial and error is cheap in simulation and expensive in the real world. Running a company works the same way. The expensive way to learn that an initiative stalled, or that a promise to a studio slipped, is to find out three weeks later. So instead of describing how I would do this job, I simulated it. One toggle flips this entire page between the Co-CEO's office as it typically runs and the same office with me in this seat. Every figure is illustrative and built from public information. All figures illustrative

The idea in one line: Runway builds world models so mistakes can happen in simulation instead of in the real world. A Chief of Staff is that layer for a Co-CEO. Plans get pressure-tested against a live model of the company, and nothing has to quietly fail for three weeks to get noticed.
