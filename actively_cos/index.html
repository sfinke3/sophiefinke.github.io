Built for the Chief of Staff role at Actively AI

Every initiative. Always worked.

Actively exists because on any given day, most accounts have zero people thinking about them. I think the same problem lives inside every company. On any given day, most of a founder's initiatives, hires, customers, and investor relationships have zero people thinking about them either. The Chief of Staff is the fix for that, so instead of describing how I would do the job, I built the system I would run it with. Every figure here is illustrative and built from public information.

The idea in one line: Actively deploys a Per-Account Agent across every account in a customer's TAM. This role deploys me across every initiative on the founders' plate, researching, surfacing what needs attention, and advancing next steps without being asked. Same thesis, pointed inward.
