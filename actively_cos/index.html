Built for the Chief of Staff role at Actively AI

Every initiative. Always worked.

Actively exists because on any given day, most accounts have zero people thinking about them. I think the same problem lives inside every company. On any given day, most of a founder's initiatives, hires, customers, and investor relationships have zero people thinking about them either. The Chief of Staff is the fix for that, so instead of describing how I would do the job, I built the instrument I would run it with. Every strategic initiative gets a card, worked the way Actively works an account, with a status, a next step, and a count of days since anyone last touched it. Underneath that sits a view of where Anshul's week actually goes, and the standing Monday brief that turns all of it into the few decisions that genuinely need a founder. Every figure is illustrative and built from public information. All figures illustrative

The idea in one line: Actively deploys a Per-Account Agent across every account in a customer's TAM. This role deploys me across every initiative on the founders' plate, researching, surfacing what needs attention, and advancing next steps without being asked. Same thesis, pointed inward.

The board · every initiative worked like an account

Per-initiative view of the founders' plate

Each card is one strategic initiative, pulled from wherever the work actually lives. The read is the same one Actively gives a rep on an account: what state it is in, what the next step is, how long it has sat untouched, and whether it truly needs a founder or can come to me. The red cards are the point. Nobody decided to drop them, they just went quiet, and a system that surfaces them is worth more than any status meeting.

The leverage view · where the week actually goes

Founder time, measured like any other scarce resource

Leverage is not a feeling, it is a ratio. This section reads the calendar and asks one question: how much of the week went to work that only a founder can do, like product direction, closing key hires, exec-level customer moments, and investors. Everything else is either mine to run or should not exist at all. The plan below is what I would take off Anshul's plate in my first month, with the hours attached.

Where Anshul's last four weeks went

Calendar hours categorized by whether the work required a founder. The 46% only-founder share is the number this role exists to move. Every hour in the middle two segments is an hour I can give back.

The first-month give-back plan

Take over the weekly exec cadence prep and follow-through (~4 hrs/wk), own first drafts of board and customer decks (~3 hrs/wk), run recruiting coordination up to the founder-close call (~3 hrs/wk), and absorb the internal status-chasing entirely (~2 hrs/wk). That is roughly 12 hours a week back, which moves the only-founder share from 46% toward the 70% target without hiring anyone else.

Turning the board into action

The Monday brief

A board full of cards still leaves someone to decide what to do about them, and that is the part of the job I care most about. Every Monday the founders get one short note drafted from the live board: what moved, what went quiet, and the few decisions that genuinely need them this week. Everything else, I just handle.
