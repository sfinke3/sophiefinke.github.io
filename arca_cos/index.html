    How we finance the next firm

    Recurring fee revenue is financeable. A facility carries the debt, equity is the down payment. Levers from RBC's index desk and Amazon FP&A.

    Average firm AUM$1.2B
    Blended fee on assets0.75%
    Purchase multiple on EBITDA8.0×
    Debt advance on the facility60%
    Equity to deploy$64M
    EBITDA assumed at 30% of fee revenue, a standard RIA margin. Advance at 0% is the all-equity case the facility replaces.
    Price per firm
    EBITDA × multiple
    Equity needed per firm
    price less what the facility carries

    Firms with the facility
